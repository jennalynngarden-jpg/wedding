    FAQs

    Which airport should I fly into?

    Monterey Regional Airport (MRY) is the closest and most convenient option. It's just 20 minutes from downtown Monterey, which is where the bulk of the hotels are. If you're looking for more flight options or better fares, San Jose (SJC) and San Francisco (SFO) are about 1.5 and 2 hours away (without traffic), respectively.

    Do I need a rental car?

    If you fly into SFO or SJC, you'll probably want one, since it's a bit of a trek down to Monterey. You could probably technically try to do rideshare down to Monterey, but it would be expensive. Caltrain runs through Gilroy, so you could also take the train down to Gilroy and then rideshare from there, but it would still be pretty pricey.

    We will have shuttles to take folks to and from the venue the day of the wedding, so if you fly into MRY and want to just do rideshare / public buses, you could get away without a car. Many guests are local to the area as well, so if you're in need of rides Jenna and Liam can connect with someone who could help you out!

    What's the attire?

    We'd like folks to aim for cocktail or semi-formal attire. Keep in mind that July evenings can be cooler so definitely bring a jacket or wrap. If you're looking for inspiration, our color palette includes sage green, warm tan, earthy brown, and forest green, but there's no pressure to match. Please just wear whatever makes you feel your best!

    Are kids allowed?

    Yes! Otherwise, more than half of Jenna's family wouldn't be able to attend haha. We're going to try to have things for kiddos to do.

    Is there parking at the venue?

    Yes! Fairview Laguna Seca has free parking on site.

    Is there a hotel room block?

    Yes! We have a room block at the Portola Hotel & Spa in downtown Monterey, right by Fisherman's Wharf. The group rate is $309 a night (plus tax) for Friday, July 23 and Saturday, July 24. To book, use our group booking link or call the hotel at 1-866-711-1734 and mention the Parrish Garden Wedding.

    The block has a limited number of rooms, and the group rate is available until June 23, 2027 or until the block fills up. If you want to stay longer after the wedding weekend, the hotel will give you the group rate for extra nights if they have rooms. The days before the wedding will be pricier, though, because of a convention in town (Thursday night is $439, and Wednesday is sold out). If you already booked at the Portola, you'll need to cancel and rebook through our link to get the group rate.

    If you'd rather stay somewhere else, we recommend booking early, as July is peak season in Monterey and hotels fill up quickly. Airbnbs and VRBO can also be a good option, if you're traveling as a group. There are a lot of them since Monterey is such a popular tourist destination.

    What's the weather like in Monterey in July?

    Monterey summers are still pretty mild. Expect daytime highs in the mid-60s to low 70s. Evenings can get chilly (upper 50s), especially near the water, so be sure to bring a jacket.
